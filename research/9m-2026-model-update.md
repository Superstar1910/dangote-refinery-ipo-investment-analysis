## Critical limitations

- The complete 9M income statement, balance sheet, cash-flow statement and notes have not been obtained.
- The model normalises the implied Q3 EBITDA margin rather than treating it as permanent.
- The US$6.69bn cash balance requires reconciliation for restrictions, offer proceeds and committed expansion uses.
- Maintenance capex is forecast separately. The US$14.3bn expansion remains an option and financing obligation rather than fully funded operating value.
- Naira/USD, WACC, margins, valuation multiples and risk haircuts remain material sensitivities.
