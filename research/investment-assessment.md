# Investment Assessment

## Bottom line

The recommendation is **Do Not Subscribe at ₦525; retain on watchlist**.

The refinery has demonstrated meaningful operating scale, export capability and strong first-half 2026 profitability. The security, however, offered inadequate compensation for five connected risks: a short operating record, uncertain through-cycle refining margins, weak cash conversion during ramp-up, concentrated control and related-party exposure, and a very large residual funding requirement for the proposed capacity expansion.

## What the price asks investors to believe

At ₦525, the post-offer equity value was approximately ₦65.2tn. The price effectively required sustainable EBITDA around US$6bn at an 8× multiple, or substantial present value from unbuilt capacity. It also implied that favourable 2026 margins, high utilisation and financing access would persist with limited dilution.

## Why the underwrite passes

1. **Peak-like earnings risk.** H1 2026 was encouraging but insufficient to establish through-cycle free cash flow.
2. **Expansion funding.** IPO net proceeds covered only a small part of the disclosed expansion programme; the remaining gap was approximately US$12.72bn before overruns, financing costs and incremental working capital.
3. **Cash conversion.** Receivables and inventory absorbed approximately ₦3.54tn in H1 2026.
4. **Governance and control.** The IPO created only about 3.30% public ownership, leaving minority investors exposed to concentrated control and related-party decisions.
5. **Valuation.** More mature listed refiners offer longer operating histories, deeper liquidity and better-established governance. DPRP should therefore trade at a risk-adjusted discount on normalised earnings, not at parity with mature peers.

## Price and action framework

| Price zone | House action |
|---|---|
| Above ₦450 | Pass absent a major improvement in evidence |
| ₦380–₦450 | Re-underwrite only if operations, financing and governance de-risk materially |
| ₦320–₦380 | Watchlist / fair-value territory |
| ₦280–₦320 | Potential accumulation zone, subject to liquidity and evidence |
| Around or below ₦280 | Initial position may be considered under a constrained fractional-Kelly framework |

At approximately ₦280, the prior portfolio framework contemplated an initial 2.5% position, a normal target around 3.5% and a hard maximum of 5%, conditional on liquidity and subsequent operating evidence. At ₦525, the mathematical and practical allocation was 0%.

## Evidence that would change the view

- Audited evidence of sustainable utilisation above 85% and mid-cycle EBITDA above US$5bn.
- Normalisation of receivables, inventories and operating cash conversion.
- A committed, facility-by-facility expansion financing plan without material dilution.
- A complete debt, security and covenant bridge.
- Stronger independent oversight of related-party transactions and capital allocation.
- A market price offering an adequate margin of safety.
