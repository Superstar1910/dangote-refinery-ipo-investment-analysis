# Excluded Source Material

The following items are intentionally excluded from the repository:

- The full DPRP prospectus and application documents;
- Third-party analyst reports, paywalled journalism and proprietary datasets;
- Copyrighted peer-company filings or investor presentations;
- Raw working papers containing non-public notes or intermediate calculations;
- Material for which public redistribution rights were not established.

The analysis cites these materials where legally and analytically appropriate. Readers should obtain them directly from the issuer, regulator, exchange or original publisher.

The repository includes only original analyst-authored research, selected analyst-created graphics and short factual references needed to explain the work.
